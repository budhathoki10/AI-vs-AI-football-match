# Design system

Tokens (`apps/web/src/styles.css`): deep navy/charcoal backgrounds (`--bg #0b1220`, panels `#121e33`), emerald accent (`#10b981`), white text, muted `#8fa0ba`, subtle 1 px borders, 10 px radius, system font stack with tabular numerals for clocks/scores.

Components: `.card`, `.btn` (primary/ghost/danger/sm, disabled + busy states), form controls, `.tablewrap` tables, `.chip` status badges, `.tabs`, broadcast `.score` board, `.timeline`, `.bar` stat bars, penalty `.pk` history dots, `.stage` 3D frame with `.camtabs`, `AgentPicker` (architecture + provider + model), SVG chart components (`charts.tsx`: passing network, momentum, possession, average formation, plus heatmap and shot map in `ui.tsx`).

3D (`Humanoid.tsx`, `Court3D.tsx`, `PenaltyScene.tsx`): procedural articulated players — legs with knees, arms with forearms, torso, head with hair, boots, goalkeeper gloves — with no external assets (so no licensing questions). Gait cadence and stride are derived from the player's *measured ground speed*, never accelerated; kick swing on pass/shot/kick, sideways goalkeeper dive, celebration (arms up, bounce) for the scoring team and dejection for the conceding team after a goal (live and replay). Referee shadows play; instanced, team-coloured crowd with a gentle sway (size by quality setting); benches with seated substitutes and a marked substitution zone; goal nets.

Responsive: breakpoints 860 px (sidebar → top bar + bottom nav, single-column grids, 4:3 stage), 520 px (scoreboard stacks badge over a two-line team name) and 1100 px. Verified by the browser e2e: **no horizontal scrolling on 12 pages at 375, 390, 430, 768, 1280 and 1920 px**. Motion: framer-motion banners for goal/half-time/full-time/shootout results; `prefers-reduced-motion` disables CSS animation. Avoided: heavy glassmorphism, glow borders, decorative icons.
